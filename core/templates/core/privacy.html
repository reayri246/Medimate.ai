MEDIMATE AI · YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MediMate AI handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective October 13, 2026

01 Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, MediMate AI may handle:

  • Account information: details such as your username, email address, and profile information.
  • Health information: measurements, notes, symptoms submitted with appointment requests, and records or prescriptions you choose to upload.
  • Service activity: appointment requests and decisions, notifications, and actions needed to operate the features you use.
  • Technical information: information processed by the application and its hosting environment to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

02 How information is used

Information is used to create and manage your account, show you your health records and measurements, process appointment requests, deliver notifications and reminders, provide the assistant and document features, and maintain the security and operation of the application.

The health assistant processes submitted questions within the application and is intended to provide general information. Do not include information you do not want processed by the service.

03 Access and sharing

Your information is associated with your account. Authorized administrators may access information as needed to operate the service, manage appointments or records, respond to support needs, and maintain the application. Appointment details may be available to the staff or clinician involved in handling your request.

MediMate AI does not make your health records public. Information may also be processed by the hosting or infrastructure providers used to run the deployment, or disclosed when required by applicable law. The specific providers depend on how this application is deployed.

04 Storage and security

Information is stored by the MediMate AI deployment and is subject to its hosting, backup, and access-control configuration. The application uses account-based access controls and security measures, but no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Keep your password private, sign out on shared devices, and contact the deployment administrator promptly if you believe your account or information has been accessed improperly.

05 Retention and your choices

Information is retained in the deployment while needed to provide the service, maintain account and appointment records, or meet operational or legal requirements. Retention and deletion may depend on the administrator's configuration and applicable rules.

You can choose what health information to enter or upload. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the administrator responsible for your deployment. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate service operations.

06 Children's privacy

MediMate AI is not designed to independently collect information from children. A parent, guardian, or authorized caregiver should manage any account or health information for a child where permitted by applicable law.

07 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, its deployment, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest published version.

08 Privacy questions

For questions or privacy requests, contact the administrator responsible for your MediMate AI deployment. The administrator can explain the hosting, retention, and access practices that apply to your instance.
